    Setup

    Full body in view, camera level. Calibrate standing in anatomical position (arms at sides, palms forward) so the twin's rest pose matches yours.

    Parts the camera cannot see are drawn faded and never measured.

    Cameras 1 (primary)

    Add up to 3 more cameras on this computer, placed around you (side and back views work best). Each one's 3D skeleton is rotated onto the primary camera and averaged joint by joint, weighted by how clearly each camera sees that joint, so joints hidden from one camera are filled in by another.

      Frames are matched by the latest available frame (no hardware sync, up to one frame apart). The "disagreement" is how far each camera's aligned skeleton is from the primary's: a consistency measure, not accuracy.

      Exercise library

      Energy systems (textbook phases)

      15 s1 min

      0.0 s of continuous activity · resting

      Continuous activity = hands or feet moving faster than 0.15 m/s (camera or exercise player); resets after 10 s still. The phase shown is the textbook description for that point of an effort, quoted from OpenStax Anatomy & Physiology 2e (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). It is not a measurement of your metabolism: real contributions overlap and depend on intensity.

      Biomechanics (estimated)

      Inverse dynamics from the tracked pose, body mass and population segment parameters (). Quasi-static unless acceleration is included. The two-foot split uses the lever rule (the true split cannot be known from motion alone); the centre of pressure is placed at each foot's centre; the head centre of mass is approximated at the mid-ear point. "Group the moment requires" is the muscle group that must produce the net moment; co-contraction is not visible. Red = required group (colour scaled to 1.5 N·m/kg, not a measured activation), blue = opposing group. Green arrows = ground reaction (length = share of body weight); orange/cyan spheres at joints = net moment size (orange extensor-type, cyan flexor-type); ball = centre of mass (green over the base of support, red outside). Not validated against force plates or EMG.

      Equipment (4D)

      Loading the equipment catalogue…

      Barbells, discs, collars, rack, bench and platform use the dimensions quoted from the IWF Technical and Competition Rules 2024 and the IPF Technical Rulebook 2026 (page and quote shown, checked against the rulebook text when the catalogue is built). Other items have no governing standard: their shape is illustrative and their load is what you enter. Loads come from mechanics (weight, band stretch, cable tension, lever, sled friction) and are added to the inverse dynamics; they are estimates, not measurements.

        1 · Tracking quality (live)

        2 · Gate V1: agreement with a goniometer

        Hold a joint still, measure it with a goniometer, enter the reading, press Record. The app value is the median of the last second. Pass marks were set before any data: mean absolute difference ≤ 5° for elbow and knee, with at least 10 pairs over 2 sessions (pairs ≥ 30 min apart). Other joints are reported without a pass mark.

              Imaging: research models on open datasets

              Models trained here on public, de-identified research datasets: X-ray, CT, MRA, MRI, ultrasound and OCT (MedMNIST v3, CC BY 4.0; IXI brain MRI, CC BY-SA 3.0, healthy volunteers). Each result is the real score on that dataset's held-out test split, next to the published benchmark. Images are small (64 px or 64³), so these are research and teaching models, not clinical tools. There is no upload: it does not read your own scans and does not diagnose.
