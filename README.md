# Meridian Training 4D + Motion Lab 4D (static public site)

Live site: https://inzy0.github.io/training-4D/

Free, non-commercial, education only. Not a medical device; no diagnosis or advice.

- **Training 4D**: the training system (profile -> phase gates -> pathway -> session, exercise library, programs, drills,
  sports, team) running entirely in your browser (Pyodide) on a privacy-cleaned copy of the workbook. Your own profile
  and athletes are kept in your browser (IndexedDB) and never sent anywhere.
- **Motion Lab 4D**: a skeleton twin driven by your camera (on your device), the demo motion or the exercise player,
  with biomechanics (inverse dynamics) and **Equipment (4D)**: barbells, discs, collars, rack, bench and platform built
  to IWF TCRR 2024 / IPF Technical Rulebook 2026 dimensions (page-cited), plus dumbbells, kettlebells, medicine balls,
  sandbag, vest, ankle weights, bands, cables, landmine, sled, rings, suspension trainer, pull-up bar, cones, hurdles,
  ladder, plyo box, stability ball and foam roller. Their loads (weight, band stretch, cable tension, lever, sled
  friction) are added to the joint-moment estimates.
